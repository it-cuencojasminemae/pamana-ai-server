# Mexico–San Fernando field-verified prototype pilot

On 2026-09-27 the PAMANA project team approved a narrowly scoped `FIELD_VERIFIED`, `REAL` prototype corridor based on manually supplied transport-point coordinates and regular-commuter confirmation. This classification supports the prototype planner; it is not `AUTHORITATIVE_CURRENT` and does not claim LTFRB or LGU endorsement.

## Activated journeys

The existing `RCH-SJ-CSF-SM-ROB` route and its two directional variants are reused. Outbound is `RCH-SJ-SMROB-OUT`, from PSU frontage to the SM main-gate drop-off with signboard **SM Pampanga**. Inbound is independently modeled as `RCH-SJ-SMROB-IN`, from Robinsons Starmills Arayat Gate to PSU frontage with signboard **SAN JUAN**. It is not generated by reversing outbound, and a Magalang jeep is not represented as a PSU return service.

The transfer option uses a minimal functional route, `PILOT-PSU-MEXICO-BAYAN-TRICYCLE`, from PSU frontage to Mexico Bayan, then `PILOT-ARAYAT-SF-MEXICO-BAYAN-SM-OUT` on the existing `RCH-ARAYAT-SF-VIA-STAANA-MEXICO` parent route. Both RouteVariantStops at Mexico Bayan explicitly allow transfer. The new tricycle name is a PAMANA service identifier rather than an official route name. The selected onward prototype variant represents the verified traditional-jeep segment; the reported modern-jeep alternative and Palengke San Fernando signboard remain evidence rather than being merged into an invented route.

## Fare and service evidence

The exact regular outbound direct fare is PHP 30. The student payment was reported **around PHP 25**, so no exact amount or derived discount percentage is encoded. Mexico Bayan to SM has an exact regular fare of PHP 14 and an exact reported student payment of PHP 11. FareRule supports a base fare and percentage discounts, not exact category amounts, so PHP 11 remains in evidence and the computed student fare remains unavailable. Return and tricycle fares are unknown.

Direct service was reported as generally available only until around 4:30 PM and difficult or unavailable from around that time onward. This is not stored as an exact last trip. Vehicles may wait or fill before departure, but no ServicePattern is created because operating days, an official schedule, and a numeric headway were not supplied.

## Intentionally unknown

All transit geometry remains null. No intermediate stops, travel time, ETA, wait time, live vehicle, fleet count, exact timetable, tricycle fare, return fare, accessibility fact, road path, or additional route is inferred. Geoapify may calculate passenger walking access and egress, but it does not supply the jeepney path. Phase 16 simulated vehicles remain separate from these production records.

Planning is enabled only for the four listed nodes, three parent routes, four directional variants, eight ordered RouteVariantStops, and two regular FareRules. Unrelated Phase 4/5 research records remain unchanged and planning-disabled.
